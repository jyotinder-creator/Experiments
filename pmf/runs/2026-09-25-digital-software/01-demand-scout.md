# 01 — Demand Scout (Maya) — run 2026-09-25-digital-software

**Scope from the brief and orchestrator:** digital, online and software only. That means SaaS, micro-SaaS, plugins, extensions, APIs, apps, memberships, and physical goods only through fully automated POD. The revenue must be recurring, reach a global audience, and not grow the founder's workload as it grows. Launch budget is a few hundred dollars (up to $10k–$50k if justified). Signals from 2025–2026 get priority. Anything a general-purpose AI chatbot now does for free is penalised or dropped.

**Test applied:** Searched + Paid + Growing. All three, or it's not a market.

**Method note:** Several key sources (ahrefs.com, apps.shopify.com) were blocked by the network proxy. Figures from them are quoted from search-result snippets and marked "(snippet)". Many demand signals come from secondary sources, often vendor blogs, and I treat them as directional. A dense page of "best X tools 2026" comparison articles is itself a demand proxy: publishers only fight over keywords that convert to paid signups.

## 1. Top-line: the 5 strongest niches

1. **AI-search visibility (GEO) tracking for SMBs, agencies or a single vertical (23/25).** Brands now pay $29–$500+/mo to see whether ChatGPT, Perplexity and AI Overviews mention them. Searches for "ai search tracking" were reported up 184%, and more than 10 funded and indie competitors publish prices.
2. **E-invoicing compliance connectors for Shopify/WooCommerce merchants (Peppol / KSeF / Factur-X) (21/25).** The spend is required by law. Belgium (Jan 2026), Poland (Feb/Apr 2026, micro firms 2027) and France (receive Sep 2026, SMEs emit Sep 2027) are live, and 4M+ French firms alone are in scope. Shopify apps already charge per invoice or per month.
3. **Vertical AI receptionist or missed-call answering for trades and clinics (21/25).** Buyer ROI is very large and prices of $29–$500/mo are proven. The weak spot is accessibility and passivity: onboarding, telephony and support load are real.
4. **Continuous accessibility monitoring and fixing for Shopify/WordPress stores (EAA + ADA) (20/25).** There were 5,000+ US digital accessibility lawsuits in 2025 (about 70% against e-commerce), and EAA enforcement ramped up in 2026. The product must not be an "overlay", because the market now distrusts those.
5. **Developer "render" APIs (screenshot / HTML-to-PDF / OG image) for SaaS and AI agents (20/25).** Solo founders have verified public revenue: ScreenshotOne at $25k+ MRR with 800+ customers, and PDFShift at about $7.2k/mo. It is the most passive, code-and-SEO-driven model on this list.

Tie-break note: six niches scored 20. Render APIs and accessibility monitoring made the top 5 over the others because they fit a *solo, passive* operator best. The others carry platform-API risk (Reddit, TikTok, Airbnb), regulatory liability (therapy notes) or a strong incumbent (church content).

## 3. Evidence cards

### 1. AI-search visibility (GEO) tracker — 23
- **Demand**
  - Searches like "ai search tracking" are reported up 184% and "ai rank tracking" up 175% (Ahrefs data, quoted in snippet): https://ahrefs.com/blog/ai-search-trends/ and https://www.omnibound.ai/blog/ai-search-statistics
  - "92% of marketers plan to optimise for AI search, only 40.6% do": https://peec.ai/ai-search-geo-statistics
  - ChatGPT had 900M weekly users in Feb 2026 and AI Overviews trigger on about 48% of tracked queries: https://www.digitalauthority.me/resources/generative-engine-optimization-trends/
  - The SERP is saturated with "best AI visibility tools 2026" comparisons, a commercial-intent proxy: https://kime.ai/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools and https://www.surmado.com/blog/best-ai-visibility-tools-2026
- **Spend**
  - Otterly $29/mo (15 prompts) to $189/mo; Ahrefs Brand Radar about $828/mo minimum; Profound and Scrunch at enterprise pricing: https://wpseoai.com/blog/ai-search-visibility-tool-pricing-what-to-expect-in-2026/ and https://criticnest.com/ai-visibility-tools-pricing/
  - Peec AI €70–199/mo; Ayzeo from $39/mo for SMBs: https://ayzeo.com/blog/ai-search-visibility-tools-small-businesses
  - Published price comparison across many vendors: https://www.get-ryze.ai/blog/ai-visibility-tools-pricing-compared-2026
- **Why now:** zero-click AI answers are replacing blue links. Monitoring means querying models repeatedly across prompts and locations, which a free chatbot cannot do for you, so the value is durable.
- **Price points:** $29–$199/mo SMB; $499+/mo enterprise.
- **Niche-down angles:** "AI visibility for dentists / law firms / SaaS", white-label reports for local SEO agencies, or a Shopify app for product mentions in ChatGPT Shopping.
- **Risk:** crowded and fast-moving. Ahrefs and Semrush are bundling this. LLM API cost per tracked prompt needs modelling.

### 2. E-invoicing compliance connector for Shopify/WooCommerce — 21
- **Demand**
  - France: every VAT business must be able to receive e-invoices from 1 Sep 2026, and SMEs must issue them from Sep 2027. 4M+ companies and about 2.5B invoices/yr are in scope: https://get-flowie.com/e-invoicing/france and https://entreprendre.service-public.gouv.fr/actualites/A18953?lang=en
  - Belgium: Peppol B2B e-invoicing from 1 Jan 2026. Poland: KSeF from Feb 2026 (large firms), Apr 2026 (most others), Jan 2027 (micro firms): https://finbite.eu/en/e-invoicing-mandates-europe-2026/ and https://www.theinvoicinghub.com/understanding-poland-2026-b2b-e-invoicing-requirement/
  - YouTube explainers exist for Shopify merchants in both EN and FR: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=z9sJnoNuf3k and https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vuzuVqDujGQ
- **Spend**
  - Sufio charges a per-e-invoice fee on top of its subscription for Peppol sends: https://sufio.com/articles/shopify/e-invoicing/belgium-peppol-einvoices/
  - ElkSend (Shopify app for Peppol/Nemhandel/KSeF): https://apps.shopify.com/elksend
  - Peppol Box from €5/mo: https://www.peppol-box.be/en/
  - Billova (https://billova.io/en/peppol-compliance) and Peppol Plugged (https://peppolplugged.com/)
- **Why now:** mandates roll across the EU from 2026 to 2028 (and ViDA from 2030). Because the spend is legally required, churn is low.
- **Price points:** €5–€30/mo plus per-invoice fees (roughly €0.10–0.50, *estimate*).
- **Risk:** you need a certified access point or approved-platform partner to resell (build on their API). Each country has its own format, and accounting suites (Odoo, Zoho, Exact) are bundling this.

### 4. Accessibility monitoring and remediation (not an overlay) — 20
- **Demand**
  - 3,117 federal web-accessibility suits in 2025 (+27% vs 2024) and 5,000+ counting state courts. About 70% hit e-commerce. The 2026 projection is about 6,176 suits: https://blog.usablenet.com/ada-web-lawsuit-trends-2026 and https://info.usablenet.com/2025-year-end-report-on-web-accessibility-lawsuits
  - EAA fully enforceable since Jun 2025, audits scaling in 2026, applies to non-EU sellers: https://www.fudge.ai/blog/european-accessibility-act-shopify/ and https://testparty.ai/blog/eaa-compliance-shopify-european-accessibility-act
- **Spend**
  - The Shopify accessibility category has many paid apps, e.g. Accessibly and AC Accessibility Toolkit: https://apps.shopify.com/accessibly-app/reviews, https://apps.shopify.com/accessibility-by-appifycommerce and https://www.storecensus.com/shopify-apps/category/accessibility
  - Venture-backed players (TestParty, accessiBe, UserWay, Recite Me) sell into Shopify: https://reciteme.com/news/shopify-eaa-compliance/
- **Why now:** lawsuits are rising and EAA is creating a new mandatory spend.
- **Price points:** $10–$50/mo SMB apps; $490+/yr for overlays (industry norm, *estimate*).
- **Risk:** overlays are explicitly said *not* to satisfy the EAA (fudge.ai above) and have drawn regulator action. The product should be a scanner plus code-level fixes for theme issues plus monitoring and a generated accessibility statement.

- **AI photo restoration/enhancer apps:** commoditised by free chatbot image models. Remini-style apps depend on weekly-subscription dark patterns and ads.
- **AI job auto-apply tools:** low trust (LazyApply 2.4/5 on Trustpilot, https://www.loopcv.pro/directory/lazyapply/), ToS/ban risk, and churn by design, since users leave once hired.
- **LinkedIn AI ghostwriting tools:** spend exists (Taplio $39–199, Supergrow $19), but the core output is what free ChatGPT does; the market is saturated.
- **Age-verification APIs:** mandatory spend is real but concentrated in adult content (founder exclusion risk), plus heavy identity/biometric compliance.
- **Notion/Canva templates and printable planners:** one-off purchases with no recurrence, and AI-generated supply floods the market.
- **AI headshots:** peaked in 2023; free image models have replaced them.
- **Transcription and generic summarisers:** free in every chatbot and OS.
- **Faceless-YouTube automation tools:** fad dynamics and YouTube's 2025 "inauthentic content" monetisation crackdown. Not verified in this run, so treat this as a known-risk flag.

- **Blocked sources:** ahrefs.com and apps.shopify.com were blocked by the proxy. Keyword growth figures (+184% / +175%) and Shopify review counts come from search snippets and secondary pages. They need checking in a keyword tool (Ahrefs/Semrush/Google Keyword Planner) before commitment.

Prices are likely accurate; market-size claims (GEO $33.7B by 2034, AI receptionist $31B by 2035) are analyst hype and were **not** used in scoring.
- **No direct review-velocity data:** I did not pull live Shopify App Store or Chrome Web Store review-velocity figures for niches #2, #4 and #12. That is the next step for the validation agent.
- **E-invoicing:** plugin pricing per country (Poland KSeF, France PA) and the difficulty of access-point partnerships are unverified.
- **Founder brief gaps:** skills, industries and audience are blank, so accessibility scores assume a technically capable solo founder. If the founder is non-technical, niches #5, #2 and #12 lose 1–2 points each.
