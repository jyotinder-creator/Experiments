# Weighted PMF Scorecard

The chair (`pmf-chair`) uses this scorecard to compare finalist niches. Score each criterion
from 0 to 10, multiply by its weight, and add the results up. The maximum is 100.
The weights favor **recurring, low-maintenance revenue** over raw market size.

| # | Criterion | Weight | Source agent | A 10/10 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Proven demand (searched and paid) | 15% | demand-scout | High-intent searches plus many competitors visibly making money |
| 2 | Gap strength (the wedge) | 15% | gap-hunter | A frequent, painful, money-losing gap that incumbents structurally won't fix |
| 3 | Customer clarity and willingness to pay | 10% | customer-psychologist | Sharp ICP, clear triggers, WTP well above the price |
| 4 | Recurring-revenue fit | 15% | customer-psychologist + offer-architect | The need naturally repeats; month 12 is as valuable as month 1 |
| 5 | Unit economics | 10% | offer-architect | Gross margin > 70% (digital) or > 40% (physical); LTV:CAC ≥ 3; payback < 3 months |
| 6 | Passivity (founder hours at steady state) | 15% | ops-automation-engineer | Under 5 hrs/week at 500 customers |
| 7 | Compounding acquisition | 10% | growth-strategist | Owned channels (SEO, marketplace rank, referrals) drive most growth |
| 8 | Risk-adjusted durability | 10% | risk-auditor | Low platform, AI-disruption and regulatory risk; survives the stress test |

**Gates, which override the score:**
- Risk-auditor verdict RED → excluded (unless the chair overrules it with a written reason).
- Passivity score < 4/10 → excluded, because it would be a job rather than passive income.
- No differentiated offer from offer-architect → excluded, because it would be a copycat.

**Interpretation:** 75 or more: go straight to validation. 60–74: validate, with extra caution on the weakest criterion. Under 60: park it.
